# Changelog

Every release needs a section here, headed `## <version> - <date>`, before it's tagged. The release workflow puts that section in the GitHub release notes and fails without it.

## 0.44.0 - 2026-11-14

- Color frames, in Settings > Editor > Layers, colors each frame in the layers panel, with a guide in its color down everything inside it, so you can see where each one starts and ends. Pick Rainbow or a palette: Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Aurora, Candy or Ember. Tint rows also washes each row in its frame's color, hover and selection included.
- Settings has a search box, and its pages are regrouped into General, Editor, Images and AI Tools, with shorter descriptions.
- Ligma keeps where you were in each file across restarts, and a crash while saving no longer loses your tabs.
- Upscale reads images in fewer, larger tiles on your graphics card, so it's faster and uses less of the card, and picks Photo or Illustration for each image by how it looks. Its cancel button works while it's still picking.
- Heal no longer shows heals you've undone while a new stroke is healing, and tells you if the image changed and needs healing again.

## 0.43.1 - 2026-11-13

- Icons only in the image bar hides the buttons' names again.

## 0.43.0 - 2026-11-13

- Heal in the image bar paints out spots, blemishes, stray objects or text in an image, on your computer. Scrub evens a spot out from what's around it instead, for text or marks on smooth surfaces. `[` and `]` resize the brush.
- Upscale in the image bar makes an image 2 or 4 times as large and sharper, on your graphics card where it can, on Windows and on Macs with Apple silicon. Pick Fast, Photo or Illustration, and compare before and after on the canvas before you apply it. Each model downloads the first time you use it.
- Icons only in the image bar, in Settings, shows the image bar's buttons as icons with their names as tooltips.
- Each team in Home has a page of its own, opened from Settings in the team's menu: its people and their roles, its projects, its plan and seats, its settings for AI, UI kits and libraries, and transferring or deleting it, each in a window of its own.
- You can make a team member an admin, also on Starter and Professional teams, and change their role from the team's page or window.
- Inviting people to a team suggests people as you type their email, and team members' emails are blurred until you click them.
- A team's projects are listed by most recent, in a grid or a list, with larger cards. The Starred page has a larger grid too.
- The Starred row on Home slides sideways when there are more files than fit. Swipe it, use the arrows on its edges, or hold a file near an edge while reordering.
- Home goes back and forward with your mouse's side buttons, Alt+Left and Alt+Right (Cmd+[ and Cmd+] on a Mac) or a sideways swipe.
- Folder colors are brighter and easier to tell apart.
- Eight new app icons: Glacier, Chroma, Petal, Sherbet, Lullaby, Scribe, Interface and Ligmerity.

## 0.28.0 - 2026-10-30

- Select object in free projects: Ligma's own Select object takes the place of Select area in the bar above the toolbar for a selected image, and picks out objects in it as Photoshop's Object Selection does. Point at an object to see it outlined, click to pick it, Shift-click to add another, Alt-click (Option-click) to take one away, or drag a box around one. Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z) undoes picks while it's open. Isolate, or Enter, puts what's picked in a new layer right above, cropped to it and transparent around it, as one undo step, leaving the image alone. Settings > Features > Images.
- It runs on your device with SAM 2.1, which Ligma asks before downloading (74 MB) the first time. Settings > Features > Images > Object model downloads it ahead of time or removes it. Windows and Macs with Apple silicon on macOS 14 and later.

## 0.27.0 - 2026-10-30

- Vectorize in free projects: Ligma's own Vectorize joins the bar above the toolbar for a selected image, after Remove background, and runs on your device on Mac and Windows with nothing to download. It traces the image into flat-colored vector shapes in a frame right under the layer, which is hidden, as Figma's does, as one undo step. Edges follow the image and antialiasing is split halfway, so logos keep their own colors with clean outlines, and photos become flat tones without halos. Settings > Features > Images.
- After vectorizing, a bar like Figma's recolors the shapes in full color, grayscale or black and white, and merges them down to fewer colors, those that look most alike first. Each change is an undo step.
